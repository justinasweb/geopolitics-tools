## What the site is

geopolitics.tools shows how countries depend on one another: who produces and buys oil, gas, food, minerals and arms, who has AI computing power, where people are displaced, and how sanctions and lending connect countries. Every number on the site shows its source and the date we last refreshed it.

It is free to use, has no accounts, no comments, no ads and no cookies.

## Where the figures come from

The figures come from official and research sources such as the World Bank, the UN, FAOSTAT, the US Geological Survey, SIPRI and UNHCR. The [Sources](/sources) page lists every one, with its licence, how often we check it and when it last changed. [Methodology](/methodology) explains how each measure is worked out.

The site does not rank countries by merit. A rank is a position on one measure, largest first.
