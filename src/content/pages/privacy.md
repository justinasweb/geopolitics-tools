## In short

geopolitics.tools has no accounts, no comments, no ads and sets no cookies. It does not use social media widgets or anything else that follows you from site to site.

## Page counts

We count visits with Fathom Analytics, which sets no cookies and stores no personal data. It records which pages are read, which site sent the visitor, and the country, browser and device type, as totals only.

## Server logs

The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, its servers see your IP address and browser details to deliver each page, and may keep them briefly in logs to keep the service running and protect it from abuse.
