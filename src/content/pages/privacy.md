## In short

geopolitics.tools has no accounts, no comments, no ads and sets no cookies. It does not use social media widgets or anything else that follows you from site to site.

## Page counts

We count visits with Fathom Analytics, which sets no cookies and does not store your IP address. To count visitors without knowing who they are, it turns each visit into a code that changes every day. It records which pages are read, which site sent the visitor, and the country, browser and device type, and we see only totals.

## Server logs

The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, its servers see your IP address and browser details to deliver each page. Cloudflare handles this data for us as a processor, may keep it in logs for a limited time to run the service and protect it from abuse, and describes this in its [privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Correction reports

If you report a correction, the form sends what you write, the page link and your email (if you give one) to the editor by Telegram message; the site itself stores none of it. Your email is used only to reply to you. Reports are kept in the editor’s Telegram and deleted within 90 days.

## Sharing and links

The share buttons are plain links to X and Telegram; nothing loads from those sites until you click. Links to sources take you to their own sites, which have their own privacy terms.

## Contact

geopolitics.tools is run by Justinas Dimsta, a private individual working under an individual activity certificate in Lithuania. He decides how the data described on this page is used (in data protection terms, he is the controller). For privacy questions, write to data@geopolitics.tools.

If you think your data has been mishandled, you can also complain to Lithuania's State Data Protection Inspectorate ([vdai.lrv.lt](https://vdai.lrv.lt/en/)).
